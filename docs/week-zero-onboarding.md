# Welcome to MyRealProduct

You are about to build one real AI product over four weeks. Each week adds a new layer to the same product: application, your data, agentic action, then production readiness.

Before our first Saturday session, please complete this Week 0 checklist. Allow around 60–90 minutes.

## 1. Join your private support group

- Join your personal WhatsApp group: **[PASTE WHATSAPP INVITE]**
- Send a short introduction with your role, current AI experience, and timezone.
- Use this group for questions, blockers, and progress updates during the program.

## 2. Open your training workspace

- Create your account: **[PASTE APP URL]**
- Confirm your name, email, and local timezone.
- Read the four-week roadmap so you understand what you will ship.

## 3. Book your first Saturday checkpoint

- Choose your one-to-one session: **[PASTE CAL.COM SATURDAY LINK]**
- Cal.com will add the appointment to your calendar and create the Google Meet link.
- Saturday checkpoints are required. If you need to reschedule, do it as early as possible.

## 4. Prepare your builder toolkit

- Create or confirm your GitHub account.
- Install Git and confirm `git --version` works.
- Install Python and confirm `python --version` or `python3 --version` works.
- Install Node.js and confirm `node --version` works.
- Install Cursor or Claude Code and open a small test project.
- Make sure you can create a repository and push one commit to GitHub.

Do not spend hours fixing setup alone. Post the error and a screenshot in WhatsApp if you are blocked for more than 20 minutes.

## 5. Bring three problems worth solving

For each idea, write three short lines:

1. Who has the problem?
2. What is frustrating, expensive, slow, or repetitive today?
3. What is the smallest useful result an AI product could deliver?

Pick problems you understand personally. A narrow, real problem is more valuable than an impressive but vague idea.

If you are stuck, start with one of these directions:

- Answer questions from a company handbook with citations.
- Compare evidence across research reports.
- Draft grounded customer-support replies.
- Turn meeting notes into approved follow-up actions.
- Evaluate a portfolio against a target role.
- Extract and validate information from incoming documents.

## 6. Draft your one-page product brief

Choose your strongest idea and write:

- **Working title**
- **Primary user**
- **Problem**
- **Proposed AI-assisted workflow**
- **Smallest useful outcome**
- **Data or documents it may need**
- **One thing you are unsure about**

This is a draft, not a commitment. We will shape and reduce the scope together during your first Saturday checkpoint.

## You are ready when

- You have joined WhatsApp.
- Your training account works.
- Your Saturday session is booked.
- GitHub and your local tools work.
- You have three problem ideas.
- You have one short product brief to discuss.

Bring questions. Week 0 is about removing friction—not proving that you already know everything.
