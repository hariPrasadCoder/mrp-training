The weekly rhythm

Saturday · required 1:1

Review the prior submission, introduce the next outcome, resolve scope questions, and leave with a clear plan.

Days 1–2 · learn

Complete the required recorded lessons. Optional depth never blocks progress.

Day 2/3 · optional office hour

Book a 30-minute architecture and ideation session through Cal.com.

Days 3–6 · build

Follow a small implementation checklist, log blockers, and update the product.

Before Saturday · submit

Send live URL, repository URL, written reflection and blockers. Hari reviews against the rubric.

Scheduling design

Cal.com owns booking

Connect Hari’s Google Calendar and Google Meet. Use a private 60-minute Saturday Checkpoint event and 30-minute weekday Office Hour event. Cal.com generates a unique Meet link.

The app owns the experience

Embed booking, show learner-local times, booked state, reschedule/cancel controls and Meet join button. Webhooks sync the booking into Neon.

Visual direction

Use the reference’s cream dotted paper, heavy black display type, thick outlines, clipped editorial panels, and restrained pink, blue, yellow and green accents. Blocks physically stack as progress grows.

Avoid

Generic LMS styling, a toy-like Lego clone, heavy 3D, points, leaderboards, decorative motion and endless identical cards.

Four implementation slices

1 · Foundation

App shell, design system, auth, Neon schema, seeded curriculum and role routes.

Done: learner and admin see different shells.

2 · Learner loop

Home, roadmap, locks, week workspace, progress, capstone and submission.

Done: Week 1 works end-to-end.

3 · Coach loop

Dashboard, learner detail, releases, review queue, rubric feedback and resubmission.

Done: Hari can run a full weekly cycle.

4 · Operations

Invites, staggered starts, timezones, Cal.com embed/webhooks, reminders and risk states.
